Privacy & community guidelines

What we collect

Rebuild stores your account email and identifier, profile name, photo, sobriety date, and information you choose to enter: check-ins, goals, journals and recordings, craving logs, movement activity, community content, reactions, partner requests, and messages. Reports and blocking records support community moderation. Enabling notifications may store a device notification token.

How we use it

We use information to provide your account, account recovery, progress, saved entries, community and partner features, notifications you enable, and support or abuse handling. We do not sell your data. Creating an account does not authorize unrelated business advertising. We do not use sobriety or journal information to target advertising.

Who can see it

Approved community posts, comments, and shared photos are visible to other signed-in users. Your display name, photo, and sobriety progress may appear with your activity. Opting into peer matching makes your peer profile discoverable. Accepted partners can see daily check-in status and messages you send them. Private journals, recordings, and craving logs are not shared with the community. Authorized administrators and service providers may access data as needed to operate the service, handle support, or meet legal requirements. Do not post information you want to keep private.

Providers and device storage

Supabase provides account authentication, database, and file storage. Expo and Apple provide push notification delivery. Notification previews depend on your device settings. Rebuild caches minimal progress on your device for offline use; this progress cache does not include journal or craving content. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Permissions and choices

Microphone access is for voice journaling. Camera and photo access are for images you choose to share. You can decline or revoke permissions in device settings. Reminders and peer matching are optional. Use post options to report concerns or block a person.

Retention and deletion

Data remains while your account is active unless removed. Use You → Delete Account to permanently delete your account and associated app data, including uploaded files and community content. Files are removed before account deletion completes. If a step fails, retry or contact support. Residual copies may remain temporarily in provider backups or where legally required.

Community rules

Posts and comments are reviewed before appearing publicly. No harassment, hate, threats, explicit sexual content, sales pitches, scams, substance sales, dangerous medical instructions, or sharing another person’s private information. Report a thread using its post options; email support for a comment or private-message concern. Peer support is voluntary, and replies are not guaranteed.

About recovery support

Rebuild is a free tracking and peer-support tool, not medical treatment, counseling, or a monitored emergency service. Alcohol withdrawal can be dangerous; contact a qualified clinician for help stopping safely. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
